About

Ben Doyle

Ben Doyle is the founder of Susurrus, drawing on a 25-year career in information security. For 21 of those years he was Chief Information Security Officer for Thales Australia and New Zealand, running security governance, risk, and incident response across enterprise, operational technology, and classified networks for a global aerospace and defence group. Ben was recognised as the longest tenured CISO in Australia before stepping into independent mentoring, angel investing and advisory work. Ben is a long-standing member of a number of advisory roles, and a co-founder of Harmonia, supporting greater inclusion across the cyber security industry. Ben has also helped train over 200 CISOs in Australia through the AISA/RSAC CISO Bootcamp. Through Susurrus, he brings that same calm, considered approach to organisations navigating cyber risk today.
